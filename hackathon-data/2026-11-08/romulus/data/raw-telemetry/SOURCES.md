# Raw telemetry samples: sources

All files were fetched on **2026-11-07** (PST) without logging in, and none of them is gated. `data/` is gitignored
(`.gitignore` line 15, `data/`), so nothing here is committed. Downloaded files were only parsed, never executed:
parsing used `python -I` with scripts kept outside this folder. Hashes are SHA-256.

Real vs synthetic: **real** = logged from physical hardware; **synthetic** = generated by a simulator or a program.

## pod-ultrasonic/ (our STM32 sensor pod, HC-SR04)
- **Origin:** the user's file `~/Downloads/ultrasonic-sample.json`. Its macOS quarantine attribute says it arrived through
  Messages at 2026-11-07 18:49:06 local time. Copied unchanged with `cp -p`.
- **License:** internal team data.
- **Contents:** `{"rows": [...]}` with 300 `D` records (`type,t,mm,us,st,ts`). The MCU clock `t` runs from 66804 to 96704 ms.
  The host clock `ts` runs from 2026-11-07 18:44:08 to 18:44:37 PST. Every record has `st=ok`, `mm=49` and `us` of 285 or 286.
- **Real vs synthetic:** **real, high confidence** *(inferred)*. The evidence:
  - The format is exactly what `heal telemetry_recent kind=D ... limit=300` prints: `tools.py` returns `{"rows": ...}`, and
    `cli.py` prints it with `json.dumps(..., indent=2)` (origin/firmware 69fc292).
  - Every row matches the firmware's integer formula `(us*343+1000)/2000` (`pod/src/hcsr04.c`).
  - Once a second, at MCU `t % 1000 == 5`, the MCU time is 1 ms late and the host time is about 22 ms late. That fits a ping
    fired in the same loop pass as the health record (`main.c`), with the D line queued behind about 250 bytes of F + H
    lines at 11520 B/s (about 21.6 ms) *(inferred)*.
  - The MCU and host clocks agree to a ratio of 0.99968.
- **Limits:**
  - It shows one steady state: a fixed target at about 49 mm.
  - It has no fault states and no A, H or E lines.
  - It's a baseline, not labeled training data.
- `ultrasonic-sample.json`: `b3b397c9f2dbafccef45da0d03e881ef612f2b851882dedaa0fd87c4878d393f`

## hf-lerobot-svla_so101_pickplace/ (SO-101, LeRobot)

- **License:** apache-2.0, per the Hub tags and the README.
- **Contents:**
  - LeRobot v3.0 format, `robot_type` so100_follower, 30 fps, 50 episodes, 11,939 frames.

- **Use for us:** commanded (`action`) vs actual (`observation.state`) tracking error on a healthy SO-101 *(inferred)*.

## hf-vtechsavyy7-so101-pen-pick-with_current_load/ (SO-101 with servo load)
- **URL:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/vtechsavyy7/so101-pen-pick-with_current_load

  only; don't redistribute it or train on it without the owner's permission.
- **Contents:**

- **Use for us:** the only public SO-101 load trace we found.

## forgis-factorynet/ (industrial arms, labeled faults, paired real/sim)
- **URL:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/Forgis/FactoryNet (arXiv 2605.09081)
- **License:** per subset (README "Licensing"):
  - `factorywave_*`, `real_csv/` and `sim_csv/`: MIT. That `real_csv/` and `sim_csv/` fall under the lab/synthetic MIT terms is
    *(inferred)*.
  - `voraus_*`: **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, NonCommercial and ShareAlike.**
- **Contents:**
  - `data/voraus_060.parquet`:
    - 398 rows across 4 episodes of the voraus-AD Yu-Cobot pick-and-place task.
    - Each row has 139 columns in the S-E-F-C schema: `setpoint_pos/vel/acc/torque`, `effort_current_iq/id`,
      `effort_motor_torque`, `effort_voltage`, `feedback_pos/vel/torque`, and `ctx_busvoltage` and `ctx_brake_voltage` per joint.
    - Labels are `ctx_anomaly_label` (Normal 298 rows, Anomaly 100) and `ctx_anomaly_category` (0–12).
    - The data is **real**.
  - `data/factorywave_data_episode_metadata.parquet`: 10,666 UR3 lab episodes with `fault_metadata` (fault_id), counterfactual
    links and injection times. It's metadata only.
  - The paired `real_csv/` and `sim_csv/` files for episode `219cdeb4-…`:
    - 136 columns, including joint pos/vel, target pos/vel/current, joint current, joint temperature, joint mode, TCP force,
      robot and safety mode, and voltages.
    - The real file is at 100 Hz, 89 rows. The sim file (Isaac Sim) is at 10 Hz, 27 rows.
    - The `fault` column is 0, so this is a healthy pair.
    - The **real/synthetic pair** shows what a sim-to-real gap looks like.
  - `README.md` is the dataset card. It warns that simulated motor current isn't calibrated and that fault signatures largely
    don't transfer from sim.
- Files:
  - `data/voraus_060.parquet`: `8e12ef722759ac5d220dab814f2dd984b737e6e61010c09b34c8d409f3fe67a5`
  - `data/factorywave_data_episode_metadata.parquet`: `6feab3cfd127a0cd9e1564c80159d6bd01d7309abb2ec25678fa8b4fe1f2b0e2`
  - `real_csv/219cdeb4-6c8f-4e3c-8aa2-f98f492edb0b.csv`: `c5670d3942e83bd319b45c015fd4cf8caef2199e8b6c6a192adb0af83a591bdd`

## uci-robot-execution-failures/ (UCI #138)
- **URL:** https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/138/robot+execution+failures.zip

- **Contents:**
  - Force/torque readings (Fx, Fy, Fz, Tx, Ty, Tz): 15 samples per instance over 315 ms, taken after a failure was detected.
  - There are 5 problems, all labeled:
    - LP1: 88 instances (normal, collision, obstruction, fr_collision)
    - LP2: 47 instances (front, back, left and right collisions)
    - LP3: 47 instances (ok, moved, slightly_moved, lost)
    - LP4: 117 instances
    - LP5: 164 instances
  - Only the `lp*.data` and html files were extracted. The zip also holds a SPARC ELF `a.out`, `format.c` and `robot`
    (C source), which were **not extracted** and must not be run. The extracted files had their execute bits removed.
- **Real vs synthetic:** presumed real *(inferred)*. The UCI page doesn't say whether the robot was physical or simulated.
